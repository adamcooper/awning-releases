Why Day, Evening and Night are set the way they are

Awning starts you with three settings. Each is a starting point, not a rule. Here's why each one is where it is, and how to make it yours.

SettingWarmthBrightnessIn short
Day5000K100%A slight tint. Move it to whatever looks comfortable, or turn Awning off.
Evening2700K55%Warm, and noticeably dimmer.
Night0K40%Red only, and dim.

Day: 5000K, 100%

The research gives no reason to warm your screen during the day. Most daytime light comes from windows and room lights, not your screen, and bright days seem to make you less sensitive to light in the evening (the research).

Most people find a screen white of about 5,400–7,300K looks the most natural. Day starts just below that, with a slight warmth some people find softer. It's a comfort setting, nothing more.

Make it comfortable. If Day looks too yellow, slide Warmth up until it looks right and click Save. If you'd rather have no tint at all, turn Awning off during the day. With a schedule, Awning does that for you.

Day used to be 4000K. We moved it to 5000K in October 2026, because a stronger daytime tint had nothing behind it.

Evening: 2700K, 55%

In the evening, what matters is how much blue-green light reaches your eyes. Warming the screen removes only about half of it. Dimming removes much more, so Evening does both.

By our estimate, a white page on a laptop at full backlight, as flicker-free mode keeps it, gives about 10 lux of that light at your eyes at 55%. That's around the level experts suggest for the evening (Brown 2022). Ordinary pages are darker than plain white, so most of the time it's less.

Make it comfortable. In a dim room you may like it lower. If text is hard to read, raise Brightness a little and click Save.

Evening used to be 80% brightness. By the same estimate that left about twice as much light, so we lowered it in October 2026.

Night: 0K, 40%

At 0K only the red part of each pixel is left. Red light hardly reaches the body clock's light sensors, and in lab studies it didn't lower melatonin. It isn't completely without effect, though: it can make you more alert, so at night darker is still better (the research).

Make it comfortable. Night is meant for late, in a dark room. If 40% feels too bright there, lower it and click Save.

Making them yours

Pick Day, Evening or Night in the menu and move the sliders. A bar appears under the three buttons: click Save to keep the change, or Undo to go back. Settings shows what each one is set to, and Reset brings back the values on this page.

The numbers on this page are calculations, not measurements of a real Mac (how we estimated them). Nothing on this page is medical advice.
