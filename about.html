About Awning

Awning is a free Mac app that warms and dims your screen, made by Adam Cooper.

How it was built

Awning began as a copy of Dimit, a screen-warmth app by Azizullo Temirov, released under the MIT licence. In October 2026 it was redesigned and renamed Awning. Both authors are named in its licence.

Awning is free under the same MIT licence. There's no trial, no licence key and no paid tier.

What it doesn't do

No account, no analytics and no tracking. Awning connects to the internet only if you turn on update checks, or choose Check for Updates yourself.

Awning is free.
